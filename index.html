The idea

A small experiment in what a lot of tiny, voluntary gestures can do.

ONE DOLLAR is a personal fundraising experiment. I am 16 years old and I live in Malta. I would like to buy a PlayStation 5 Pro and Grand Theft Auto VI, and this campaign is a personal goal to help me contribute toward that purchase.

Instead of asking a few people for a lot, I want to see whether 500 people around the world would be willing to give a small amount each. The campaign target is $500 USD. That is a target for this campaign, not necessarily the full price of the purchase.

This is not a charity, a business or an investment. It is one teenager, one wish list and an honest question. Nobody has to take part, and saying no is completely fine.
